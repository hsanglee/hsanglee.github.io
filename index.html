About

I am interested in efficient AI: making models smaller and faster to run, from mobile NPUs to GPUs. My work so far has centered on on-device AI, through model compression and inference optimization.

Over 15 years at LG, my work has moved from algorithms to silicon and back: picture-quality algorithms for TVs, then chip design, an industry interface standard, and AI chip architecture, and now compressing and optimizing AI models to run on that hardware. This path is why I approach on-device AI as a hardware–software co-design problem.
